About Us

Shahran Cultural & Research Center (A Unit of Shahran Foundation) is dedicated to preserving and teaching Urdu and Arabic languages. Located in Hazur Nagar, Muzaffarnagar, we offer quality education from nursery to intermediate levels.

Vision: To create a generation fluent in classical languages while staying rooted in culture.

Mission: Affordable, accessible, and excellent language education for all.
