About Us

Shahran Cultural & Research Center (A Unit of Shahran Foundation) is dedicated to providing quality Quran and Islamic education online. Located in Hazur Nagar, Muzaffarnagar, we offer a wide range of courses for all ages and levels.

Vision: To create a generation that understands and practices the Quran and Sunnah.

Mission: Affordable, accessible, and excellent Islamic education for everyone.
